### About Me
I am a 4th year undergraduate student studying Mathematics and Physics at the University of Warwick, United Kingdom. My interests are mainly in theoretical physics and the mathematical structures which emerge from it. Currently, my master's thesis is being supervised Dr Amos Chan, where we are studying aspects of quantum chaos. However, this summer I completed a research project under Dr Gareth Alexander, in theoretical biophysics, where I was thinking about the mechanics of lipid membranes. Please feel free to [browse my posts](/posts/). I will attempt to keep this blog updated with weekly reports from my dissertation work, lecture notes which I choose to formalise in LaTeX and any other tid-bits which I find the time to post.

I am currently pursuing research as a career, and am actively looking for PhD positions in the UK, Northern Europe and North America. Of course I am always open to correspondence and my contact details are available on this page.
