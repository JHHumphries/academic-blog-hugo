---
title: "Membrane Mechanics"
---

Much of biology can be fundamentally understood as the interplay between genetic infomation, physical stresses and geometry. Biological systems respond, evolve and adapt in response to geometric contraints e.g. during morphogenesis, embryonic development and in biological membranes. 

Highly succesful effective theories of lipid membranes have existed for 50 years but here we try to generalize results obtained about homogenous Canham-Helfrich membranes to include a spatially varying spontaneous curvature and also treat this case with an elastic rim. 

Sits within soft matter and theoretical biophysics and was undertaken with the supervision of [Dr Gareth Alexander](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/physics/staff/academic/galexander/) in the Department of Physics at Warwick University.
