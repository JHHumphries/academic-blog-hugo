---
title: "Membrane Mechanics"
---

The understanding of the morphology of biological structures is critical to many fields of biology. In this project I tried to generalize what is currently known about to Canham-Helfrich membranes to include a spatially varying spontaneous curvature and free rim. 

Sits within soft matter and theoretical biophysics and was undertaken wit the supervision of [Dr Gareth Alexander](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/physics/staff/academic/galexander/) in the Department of Physics at Warwick University.
