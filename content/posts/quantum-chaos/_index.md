---
title: "Quantum Chaos"
---

I am interested in the universal signatures of strongly interacting many-body quantum systems. Universality is like a metaphor, it connects disparate physical systems which may vary greatly in their characteristic scales. In particular I am working on the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev, an important toy model for holography (in String Theory) which exhibits maximal quantum chaos. Currently, I am interested in moments of the Spectral Form Factor (SFF), a diagnostic of long-range correlations, as it has been successfully shown to probe universality in quantum chaotic systems.

This field sits at the intersection of high energy, statistical mechanics, quantum infomation and condensed matter. Alongside this project, I am learning more about Quantum Field Thoery, Conformal Field Theory, Low Dimensional Gravity and Black Hole Thermodynamics.
