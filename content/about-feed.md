The ticker automatically finds papers on topics I follow. It displays paper titles, dates and sources, with links to the arXiv record or the publisher’s DOI page. It uses the [arXiv API](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/index.html) and [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/) for APS and Nature Portfolio records.

The feed is independent of those services and publishers. Its topic filters can miss relevant papers or include loosely related ones. For the full paper and its reuse terms, follow the link to the original record.

**Saved** means the headline comes from a stored result. The site shows a saved sample while it checks for fresh papers. It can also use the sample or a previously fetched result if a source is slow, unavailable or returns incomplete results. The date shown belongs to the paper.

Thank you to arXiv for use of its open access interoperability.
